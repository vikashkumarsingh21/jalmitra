The Global Water Crisis

Every day, thousands of tons of plastic waste, industrial chemicals, and organic pollutants contaminate our precious water bodies. Traditional cleanup methods are inefficient, expensive, and often cause more environmental damage. This crisis silently destroys marine ecosystems and threatens human health worldwide.

🌊 8 Million Tons

Of plastic waste enters oceans annually, creating massive pollution zones that destroy marine life.

💰 $139 Billion

Global cost of water pollution damage to economies, health systems, and environments each year.

⚠️ 80% of Wastewater

Is discharged untreated into water bodies, creating dead zones and toxic environments.

Our Revolutionary Solution

Jalmitra combines artificial intelligence, renewable energy, and advanced robotics to create an autonomous water cleaning system that operates 24/7 with minimal human intervention.

🤖 Autonomous Cleanup

AI-powered detection and collection system using computer vision and machine learning to identify and remove various types of pollutants automatically.

📊 Real-Time Monitoring

Advanced sensor network continuously tracks pH levels, turbidity, temperature, dissolved oxygen, and chemical contamination with live data streaming.

☀️ Solar Powered

100% renewable energy operation with high-efficiency solar panels and battery storage for continuous day-night functionality.

🗺️ Smart Navigation

GPS-enabled mapping system for targeted cleanup operations, path optimization, and remote monitoring capabilities.
