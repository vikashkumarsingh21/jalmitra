We Build Smarter Water Cleaning Robots

AI-driven, solar-powered, and sustainable — Jalmitra is revolutionizing water pollution control.

The Problem

Every day, thousands of tons of plastic, weeds, and chemical pollutants are dumped into rivers and lakes. Manual cleanup methods are slow, costly, and ineffective. Water pollution is silently killing marine life and threatening human health.

Our Solution

Autonomous Cleanup

Detects and collects waste using AI & OpenCV, powered by a conveyor belt system.

Real-Time Monitoring

Smart sensors collect live data on pH, gas, turbidity, TDS, and temperature.

Solar Powered

Fully sustainable with solar energy to reduce cost and carbon emissions.

Future Ready

Upcoming GPS mapping will allow targeted, location-based autonomous cleanup.

Who It Helps

Government Agencies

Automate cleanup in rivers, lakes, and ponds with real-time pollution tracking.

Environmental NGOs

Deploy eco-friendly solutions at scale and monitor progress through live data.

Tourism & Recreation

Clean water boosts local tourism, making rivers and lakes safe for activities again.

Scalable Business, Real Impact

Jalmitra isn’t just a project — it’s a scalable innovation with real market potential. Through partnerships, data subscriptions, and CSR-backed collaborations, Jalmitra will generate impact and income sustainably.
